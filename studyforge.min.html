🤖 How auto-generation works

No AI, no network — a deterministic script scans your notes for patterns:

  • Term: definition, Term = …, Term - … → definition cards
  • **Term** is/are/means … → term cards
  • Q: … / A: … → question cards
  • ## Heading + paragraph → topic cards
  • Frequent key terms → fill-in-the-blank cards

Every candidate shows its rule and confidence. You approve each card before it joins a deck, and you can edit or delete anything afterwards.
